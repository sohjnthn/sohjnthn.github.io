Early Mesos Farming

Prioritise level-appropriate training maps with stable mob density to obtain equipment drops. Unused equipment should be processed via Ardentmill extraction into item crystals, which can then be sold through the Maple Auction for Mesos.

Make use of permanent systems:

  • 1. Maple Auction (selling item crystals and materials)
  • 2. Ardentmill professions (Alchemy (requires learning Herbalism first) - extraction and material conversion)
  • 3. Daily bossing (Glowing SOul Crystal drops for direct Mesos)

Avoid unnecessary spending, and instead build Mesos steadily through regular farming, consistent extraction, and monitoring market prices before selling.

The overall intent is to maintain a self-sustaining Mesos flow without reliance on Cash Shop systems.

Efficient Gear Progression

Progress equipment using naturally obtained gear, boss drops, and system-based upgrades, without dependence on Cash Shop boosts which require spending real money (legal tender) for @Cash.

Follow a structured upgrade path:

Early-term → Fensalir equipment and expedition boss drops

Mid-term → Only for the main character, purchase the following cheapest, clean (non-spell trace, star force, normal potential, and additional potential-enhanced; but can have additional stats in blue text) equipment via the Maple Auction with Mesos (as the Meso costs for leveling up the Arcane and Authentic Symbols are very high)

  • 1. lv200 Arcaneshade hat, shoes, gloves, cape, and shoulder decoration
  • 2. lv150 Root Abyss top, and bottom

Long-term → Long-term → Gradual Mesos-based upgrades through enhancement systems

Enhance (using the event scroll for providing Unique normal potential with a 100% chance for untradable equipment), star force (using the event scrolls for providing 20 or less stars to untradable equipment for up to lv160), and potential your equipment only using Mesos, focusing on cost-efficiency (using 100% passing chance spell trace scrolls) rather than perfection.

Make full use of Maple Union (including Maple Union Artifact); and link skills to strengthen your overall account instead of overspending on a single character.

Daily Bossing

Clear daily (at least until Normal Papulatus, Normal Arkarium, Hard Von Leon, Normal Magnus, and Normal Pink Bean can be defeated with about up to three times of using the lv9 VI version of the 4th (if not 3rd; or 2nd) job main attack skill without cooldowun duration) expedition bosses consistently to generate stable Mesos income and obtain useful drops such as equipment, cubes, and crafting materials.

Start with easier expedition bosses:

  • Requires at least 0.6m to 1.0m clean damage range (the lower to the higher numbers are the Stat ATT as compared to the Combat Power for the Character Info window): Normal Zakum
  • Requires at least 2.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Hilla
  • Requires at least 2.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Ranmaru
  • Requires at least 2.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Horntail
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range before carrying out the 6th job advancement: Normal Von Bon
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range before carrying out the 6th job advancement: Normal Pierre
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range before carrying out the 6th job advancement: Normal Crimson Queen
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range before carrying out the 6th job advancement: Normal Vellum

Progress into core expedition bosses (it is safer to fight the following expedition boss monsters after carrying out the 6th job advancment; and after reaching lv11 for the Authentic Symbols for Cernium, and Arcs), with the (at least lv9) VI version of the 4th (if not 3rd, or 2nd) main attack skill which does not have cooldown duration:

  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Papulatus
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Magnus
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range: Normal Arkarium
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range: Hard Von Leon
  • Requires at least 19.0m lower clean damage range (remember to use the first 6th job attack skill (which is not the later released Ascent skill) first; then to attack with the (at least lv9) VI version of the main attack skill without cooldown duration): Normal Pink Bean

These bosses provide Glowing Soul Crystals for direct Mesos via the NPC Collector in the Free Market (which can be accessed via Ardentmill), expedition boss accessory set equipment drops for progression; or equipment (which can only be sold for 1 Meso each to an NPC shop) extraction, and additional materials for crafting and selling.

Even lower-tier bosses contribute consistently over time. Focus on routine completion rather than difficulty scaling.

Bossing should be treated as a daily Mesos generation system, integrated with Maple Auction selling, Ardentmill extraction, and overall account progression systems.
