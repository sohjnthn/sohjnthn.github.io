Early Mesos Farming

Prioritise level-appropriate training maps with stable mob density to obtain equipment drops. Unused equipment should be processed via Ardentmill extraction into item crystals.

Make use of permanent systems:

  • 1. Maple Auction (selling item crystals and materials)
  • 2. Ardentmill professions (Alchemy - extraction and material conversion)
  • 3. Daily bossing (Glowing Soul Crystal drops for direct Mesos)

Avoid unnecessary spending, and instead build Mesos steadily through regular farming, consistent extraction, and monitoring market prices before selling.

Efficient Gear Progression

Progress equipment using naturally obtained gear, boss drops, and system-based upgrades.

Follow a structured upgrade path:

Early-term → Fensalir equipment and expedition boss drops

Mid-term → For the main character, purchase:

  • 1. lv200 Arcaneshade hat, shoes, gloves, cape, and shoulder decoration
  • 2. lv150 Root Abyss top, and bottom

Long-term → Gradual Mesos-based upgrades through enhancement systems

Make full use of Maple Union and link skills to strengthen your overall account.

Daily Bossing

Clear daily bosses to generate consistent Mesos and build progression.

Start with easier expedition bosses:

  • Normal Zakum - Requires at least 0.6m to 1.0m clean damage range (for the Character Info window - Stat ATT as compared to Combat Power)
  • Normal Hilla, Normal Ranmaru, and Normal Horntail - Requires at least 2.0m lower clean damage range
  • Normal Von Bon, Normal Pierre, Normal Crimson Queen, and Normal Vellum - Requires at least 19.5m lower clean damage range before 6th job

Progress into more difficult non-Chaos mode expedition bosses after 6th job advancement:

  • Hard Von Leon, Normal Arkarium, and Normal Papulatus - Requires at least 19.5m clean damage range, with the at least lv9 VI version for the 5th job attack skill which causes the highest total % skill damage for all hits for the initial attack.
  • Normal Magnus - Requires at least 19.5m clean damage range, with the at least lv9 VI version for the 4th (if not 3rd; or 2nd accordingly) job main attack skill without cooldown duration.
  • Normal Pink Bean - Requires at least 19.5m clean damage range, with the at least lv9 first 6th job attack skill; then the at least lv9 VI version for the 4th (if not 3rd; or 2nd accordingly) job main attack skill without cooldown duration.
  • Hard Ranmaru, Normal Kawoong, Easy Cygnus, and Normal Cygnus - Requires at least 19.5m clean damage range, with the at least lv9 Ascent skill (which is the second 6th job attack skill) which causes the highest total % skill damage for all hits for the initial attack.

Even lower-tier bosses contribute consistently over time. Focus on routine completion rather than difficulty scaling.
